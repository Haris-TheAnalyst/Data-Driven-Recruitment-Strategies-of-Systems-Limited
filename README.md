# Data-Driven Hiring at Systems Limited

## What is this project?

This is a research project on **Systems Limited**, a large Pakistan-based IT company, studying how they use **data and technology** to hire employees — instead of relying on old-style "gut feeling" hiring.

## Why did we do this?

Hiring the wrong person is expensive and disruptive for any company. We wanted to research a real organization and see how a modern HR tool actually replaces guesswork with data, and whether the theory (what researchers say about data-driven hiring) matches the reality (what a real company actually does).

## About the Research

- **Company studied:** Systems Limited — a global IT company based in Pakistan, and a certified SAP Platinum Partner
- **Tool studied:** SAP SuccessFactors (Recruiting Management module)
- **Why this company:** They hire large numbers of technical staff and cannot afford bad hires, so they rely heavily on structured, data-based hiring instead of manual processes

## Our Research Process (Step by Step)

### Step 1: Reviewed the Literature
- Looked at what researchers (Cappelli, Boudreau & Cascio, Ulrich & Dulebohn, Marler & Boudreau) say about data-driven hiring and why human "gut feeling" hiring is often unreliable due to cognitive bias

### Step 2: Researched the Company
- Studied Systems Limited's background, scale, and why a modern hiring system is essential for them

### Step 3: Mapped the Hiring Process
- Broke down how the company's SAP SuccessFactors tool works, step by step:
  1. **Job Request** — managers submit a digital requisition instead of emails
  2. **Screening** — an Applicant Tracking System scans resumes and gives each candidate a **Fit Score** (1–100) based on required skills
  3. **Structured Interviews** — interviewers use a digital scorecard to rate candidates on Technical Skills, Teamwork, and Problem Solving, removing subjective opinions

### Step 4: Analyzed the "Predictive Loop"
- Looked at how the tool connects recruitment data with employee performance data afterward
- If a high Fit Score candidate becomes a high performer, the system is working
- If not, HR adjusts the scoring rules — so the system keeps improving over time

### Step 5: Weighed the Pros and Cons
- **Benefits:** faster hiring, consistent standards across global offices, and transparent, data-backed decisions
- **Limitations:** the tool can inherit bias from old hiring data, it's expensive, it's complex to use properly, and it requires strong data security

### Step 6: Wrote Up the Findings
- Summarized everything into a report and presentation with a clear conclusion

## Key Takeaway

For a large-scale company like Systems Limited, digital, data-driven hiring isn't optional — it's necessary. Data-based decisions turn out to be fairer, faster, and cheaper than relying on gut feeling, but the tool is only as good as the humans and data behind it.
